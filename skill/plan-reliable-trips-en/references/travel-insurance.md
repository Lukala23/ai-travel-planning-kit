# Essential travel-insurance reminders

Read this complete module only on the insurance triggers in `SKILL.md`; an ordinary international task may give one relevant reminder without loading the full file. Assume the traveler decides on and buys whole-trip insurance independently. Do not proactively rank products, compare complete policies clause by clause, build a comprehensive gap table, or collect extensive insurance information.

## 1. Confirm only minimum status

- Ask only whether insurance is `Purchased / Traveler plans to arrange / Undecided`. Once the traveler says they will arrange it, do not repeat sales-like prompts or request product detail.
- Never request policy, card, account, full medical-record, or identity-document numbers. The traveler enters underwriting information directly with the insurer.
- Without applicable policy wording, do not claim that an event is covered or will be paid.

## 2. Flag only easy-to-miss trip-specific conditions

Select genuinely relevant items, normally no more than three to five:

- **Mandatory evidence**: visa, entry, cruise, tour, or activity certificate requirements, including territory, dates, minimum elements, and language;
- **Special activity**: explicit exclusion, altitude/depth ceiling, qualification, search-and-rescue, or helicopter condition for high-altitude hiking, skiing, diving, motorcycle use, self-drive, racing, or remote travel;
- **Remote care and evacuation**: possible referral, evacuation, or repatriation beyond ordinary local treatment;
- **Pre-existing condition or pregnancy**: mention the applicable wording only when the traveler raises it; do not request a full medical history;
- **Large non-refundable commitment**: suggest considering cancellation/interruption only when the amount materially matters;
- **Credit-card benefit**: only when the traveler plans to rely on it, flag payment activation, covered travelers, trip duration, and major exclusions;
- **Rental-vehicle protection**: review under `self-drive.md`; CDW/LDW is not whole-trip insurance.

Do not maintain a static list of countries that “always require insurance.” Verify mandatory evidence and activity requirements for the current dates from the responsible authority or operator.

## 3. Give minimum usage reminders

- Save the certificate, 24-hour assistance number, emergency/pre-authorisation steps, and official claim channel offline before departure.
- In an incident, protect personal safety first; when safe, contact assistance and preserve relevant medical bills, carrier reports, police records, or cancellation evidence.
- Never guarantee a claim or suggest exaggerating, changing, or fabricating an event description.

## 4. Output boundary

Default to `No extra insurance reminder / Confirm before departure / Traveler plans to manage whole-trip insurance` plus a few trip-specific notes. Do not produce product recommendations or a large table.

Only when the user explicitly asks and provides redacted wording, help locate limitations directly relevant to the trip. State that the insurer's formal written answer controls; do not replace insurance sales, legal, or claims advice.
