# Accommodation research, area planning, and recommendations

Read only when selecting or recommending accommodation, comparing areas/properties, or auditing a booking that may change route or cost. Every overnight route still identifies lodging status and base, but a settled booking with no material mismatch does not require the full research rules or an alternative ranking.

## 1. Define the current accommodation task

- Extract check-in/out, nights, travelers, rooms, beds, child policy, budget unit, type, booking status, and non-negotiables.
- Determine whether the task is area choice, exact-property recommendation, channel comparison, booked-property review, or an experiential multi-base plan.
- Treat an existing booking as a route anchor; suggest replacement only for material mismatch or explicit request.
- With unknown dates/travelers/rooms, compare areas and property types but never invent availability or total.

## 2. Search areas and exact properties actively

Build area candidates from urban structure and route skeleton, then search exact properties in high-fit areas using:

- official property/group channels for room, facilities, policy, membership, and direct benefits;
- reputable regional or global booking channels the traveler can actually use and obtain support from;
- metasearch and maps for discovery, followed by a bookable direct or agency page;
- lawful official/management/specialist channels for apartments, guesthouses, huts, or special stays.

This is not a mandatory platform checklist. For finalists, compare two currently bookable channels and the official one where accessible. When login, region, cookies, dynamic pricing, or blocking prevents verification, say so; never treat a search snippet as a live fare.

Normalize:

- complete local dates;
- adults, children, rooms, and occupancy;
- exact room, bed, view/floor, breakfast, and benefits;
- free-cancellation deadline, prepay/pay-at-property, and refund;
- tax, service, cleaning, and display currency.

Count member/app/mobile/card/private prices only when the user qualifies. Do not compare platform rating scales directly.

## 3. Optimise area with the route

Compare meaningful areas using current nodes:

- daily door-to-door time, transfers, and cost to attractions, bookings, hubs, and meeting points;
- reliable access and safe return on arrival/departure, before sunrise, after sunset, and from night/photo nodes;
- whether the user can walk directly into a coherent, comfortable street environment, including hills, crossings, lighting, and weather;
- genuinely open nearby streets, views, markets, daily services, and food after returning;
- breakfast, dinner, local shops, food markets, convenience, pharmacy, and laundry;
- metro/bus entrance, stairs/lifts, luggage drag, taxi pickup, parking, and charging;
- noise, bars, works, crowds, accessibility, and traveler ability;
- whether cheaper rooms are offset by transport, lost time, poor walking, or higher failure risk.

Build a route skeleton, choose an area, then recompute each day and meals from the base. If it creates backtracking, unreliable late return, or weak daily life, reconsider. Explain route efficiency, city life, food, stay quality, total cost, and experiential value separately rather than hiding trade-offs in one score.

## 4. Evaluate exact properties

Verify for every finalist:

- exact address, real entrance, lawful status/registration, and searchable name;
- current exact room, occupancy, bed, private/shared bath, and whether photos match it;
- check-in/out, late arrival, desk hours, self check-in, registration, and luggage storage;
- lift/stairs, air conditioning/heating, soundproofing, smoking, hot water, data, and accessibility;
- payment time/method, deposit/pre-authorisation, cancellation, no-show, change, and channel support responsibility;
- parking/height/charging for drivers; early breakfast, gear storage/drying, transfer, and early checkout for hikers;
- real path from entrance to station, sights, dining, and night activity—not straight-line distance.

Treat view, balcony, floor, connecting room, and transfer as guaranteed only for the exact booked room/property confirmation; a request note is not a promise.

## 5. Experiential accommodation as a trip node

- `Functional base`: sleep, transport, daily convenience, and cost.
- `Experiential stay`: irreplaceable view, architecture, hot spring, history, local life, wildlife, photography, or social-sharing value worth formal route time.

For a mountain sunrise or similar weather-dependent stay, verify whether the view is inside or at a public point, exact room/orientation/floor/balcony/common area and obstruction, date-specific sunrise direction and season, weather probability and lead time, whether marketing imagery depends on drone/telephoto/heavy edit/extreme conditions/inaccessible position, and premium/minimum stay/cancellation/residual value after weather failure.

Avoid no-value moves, but do not make minimum moves absolute. Recommend one special night or multiple bases when unique experience, route link, photo, or recovery benefit exceeds premium, packing, checkout, luggage, check-in wait, and weather risk. Compare staying versus visiting when overnight is not required.

## 6. True total and booking risk

Calculate:

- whole-stay room total, not “from per night”;
- city/accommodation tax, resort, service, cleaning, platform, and mandatory extras;
- breakfast, extra bed/child, parking/charging, storage, late check-in/out;
- deposit, pre-authorisation, payment currency, exchange, card/wallet compatibility, and international payment cost;
- public transport, taxi, airport/station transfer, and luggage movement induced by location or moves.

Show nightly and whole-stay totals, currency, per room/person, tax inclusion, channel, and verification time. A checked fare is not guaranteed.

Classify each charge rather than calling all property payment “tax”: statutory and city/tourist/accommodation tax, resort, mandatory service, cleaning, platform fee, voluntary tip, and deposit/pre-authorisation. State per person/room/night/percentage, inclusion, and payment timing. A deposit is not spent cost unless retained; give hold and release conditions/time.

For a mismatch at arrival or checkout, request an itemised bill and charge name/calculation/booking or legal basis; compare confirmation, room, and paid items; involve manager and platform where needed. Match card amount/currency before approval. Prioritise person, documents, and luggage; preserve booking, bill, and payment evidence for the appropriate consumer, tourist-police, platform, or issuer route.

When visa, transport, weather-dependent experience, or party size is unstable, show cancellable first, with non-refundable saving, deadline, and maximum loss. Do not direct private off-channel transfer.

## 7. Reviews, images, and marketing noise

- Official source proves facilities/policy; booking channel proves inventory/total/terms; recent guests reveal field experience.
- Do not recommend from aggregate score or rank. Read recent detailed room-specific, local/multilingual reviews, ordinary photos, and negative reports for repetition.
- Distinguish old/new, main/annex, before/after renovation, and room types.
- Check location mismatch, works, noise, cleanliness, pests, hot water, HVAC, lift, data, repeated cancellation, view gap, and arrival surcharge.
- Use `Higher risk` with observed reasons; never allege review fraud or scam without evidence.

## 8. Deliver a recommendation

When accommodation is unbooked and affects the route, give area conclusion before exact shortlist:

| Area / property | Role | Why it fits | Route and nearby walk | Local-food access | Experience | Comparable total | Room/rights | Cancellation/payment | Risk | Channel/time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Let destination complexity determine list length; do not create low-quality choice volume.
- Give a clear overall recommendation and only real alternatives for lower price, transport, city walking, experiential stay, or lower risk.
- Link official and verifiable booking pages and recommend a channel with reasons, but never complete an irreversible booking.
- State the dates, travelers, budget, route, and room on which the recommendation depends; rerun after they change.
- For an existing booking, deliver fit review and route/meal/budget effects, not another unrelated ranking.
