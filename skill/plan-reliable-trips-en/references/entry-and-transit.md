# Entry, International Transit, and Border Rules

Read only when the current request needs a visa, entry, international-transit, customs, or border-document decision, or when that uncertainty materially blocks the current plan. A foreign destination in the background is not enough.

This module owns whether the cross-border chain can legally be completed. `air-travel.md` owns air routing, fares, and comfort; `international-operations.md` owns payment, connectivity, and day-to-day operation abroad. A sellable ticket does not prove permission to transit or enter.

## 1. Identify the applicable traveler and chain

Extract only facts needed for the current decision:

- issuing country or territory of the passport/travel document, plus residence status only when relevant;
- origin, transit countries and airports, destination, stay length, and purpose;
- protected through ticket versus separate tickets, through-checked baggage, terminal/airport change, overnight, and any need to leave the international zone;
- exact travel dates and any traveler category that changes the rule.

Do not request passport or identity numbers, private addresses, payment data, verification codes, or unredacted document images. Never substitute rules for another nationality, airport, border post, or year.

## 2. State minimum viable requirements before feasibility

List what must be true for this exact chain before saying whether it is feasible:

- visa, visa waiver, electronic authorization, transit visa, arrival form, and permitted stay;
- passport validity/blank pages and any onward ticket, lodging, funds, invitation, or purpose evidence;
- airside versus landside transit, immigration, baggage reclaim/recheck, airport change, or repeated security;
- customs, quarantine, medicine, food, cash declaration, batteries, drones, and other items actually carried;
- mandatory insurance evidence, health declaration, vaccination certificate, or medicine documentation for boarding/entry;
- official fees, official application channel, processing time, holidays, and latest useful action date.

Mark each `Met / To confirm / Not met / Not applicable`, then conclude `Feasible / Conditionally feasible / Clearly unmet / Cannot yet determine`. Preserve pending actions and rechecks even when no blocker is found. “No issue found yet” is not an admission guarantee.

## 3. Verification method

- Use at least one direct authority or actual operator responsible for the traveler and route.
- Add a second independent source when wording is ambiguous, sources conflict, a page is inaccessible, or the downside is severe and confirmation is practical. Syndicated copies of one source are not independent.
- Prefer immigration/consular authorities, customs, the exact airport or border operator, and the operating airline as appropriate.
- Check publication/effective date, traveler identity, border point, terminal, ticket protection, and baggage conditions. Airline or third-party databases may support boarding execution but cannot override the competent authority's rule.
- For distant travel dates, state the check date and a recheck point. If dynamic pages cannot be reached, provide the official entry point and unresolved questions instead of reconstructing an answer from memory.

## 4. Border preparation and official charges

- Check only required e-visa/ETA, arrival/customs/health forms, preregistration, online check-in, and document pre-clearance.
- Separate permission, immigration, quarantine, customs, baggage reclaim/recheck, and terminal movement.
- Verify an official charge's name, amount, affected traveler, official channel, and receipt. Never claim a commercial fast-track guarantees admission, bypasses customs, or buys favorable treatment from officials.
- Research paid fast-track or assistance only when asked or when a tight connection/accessibility need makes it material, identifying the exact service component.

## 5. Unofficial demands and document safety

Warn only when official travel advice or several credible recent sources repeatedly identify a risk for a specific border, process, or traveler group. Do not generalize from one post.

- Separate verified official fees from reported unofficial demands. Never recommend or normalize payment to a public official.
- When safe, ask calmly for the charge name and legal basis, use an official counter/electronic channel, request a receipt, or ask for a supervisor.
- Do not encourage confrontation or unlawful recording. Prioritize personal and document safety; once safe, use appropriate border operator, tourist-police, embassy/consular, or emergency channels and preserve lawfully obtained details.

## 6. Transit and arrival execution

- Use complete local dates, weekdays, time zones, airport, and terminal; expose date-line changes.
- Separate check-in, security, immigration, baggage reclaim/recheck, terminal/airport transfer, and boarding.
- Do not treat minimum connection time as a personal guarantee. Distinguish protected connections from self-transfer responsibility.
- If the current question includes the first arrival connection, verify last transport, safe late-arrival lodging/transfer, and a missed-connection fallback. Otherwise do not expand into a complete local-operations guide.

## 7. Minimum output

For a narrow question use: `Conclusion -> decisive conditions -> missing facts -> minimum viable requirements -> sources and recheck date`. Do not append airfare, lodging, payment, insurance-product, or full-itinerary research unless it is in scope.
