# Core Conditional Output Rules

Read this file only when delivering candidates, a comparison, an executable route, or a revised trip plan. Each active specialist module owns its domain fields and judgments. This file defines only the minimum cross-task presentation and does not repeat every module.

## 1. Deliver only the current scope by default

- Lead with the answer: what to choose, why, and the decisive limitation.
- Show only current inputs, unknowns, and assumptions that affect the conclusion. Do not print the complete internal trip brief.
- Put direct sources and verification dates near dynamic conclusions; do not over-cite stable background.
- Expose unresolved items, pre-departure rechecks, and conditions that would change the plan.
- Do not emit sections for inactive modules or inflate a simple answer into a handbook.

## 2. Organize by task scope and delivery form

Verification strength controls evidence, not the number of sections. A narrow high-consequence answer may still be short.

### Narrow answer + chat

Use `Conclusion -> necessary basis -> next action or recheck`. Avoid a large table when a few paragraphs solve the problem.

### Current plan + chat or structured plan

Combine as needed: current conditions, recommended structure, decisive candidates, tradeoffs, cost basis, risk/unknowns, and next actions. Stop at enough candidates to decide; do not force three variants.

### Comprehensive research

Only when comprehensive coverage is explicit, add like-for-like comparison, source conflicts, failure conditions, sensitivity to important parameters, a compact evidence table, and material excluded candidates.

### Formal files

After the route is confirmed and files are explicitly requested, read `deliverable-package.md`, derive required views from one authoritative source, and run its final acceptance checks. Do not load or simulate the complete delivery checklist during an early draft.

## 3. Common result structures

### Executable route

For a genuinely timed route, this table may be used:

| Local time | Stop and purpose | Transport/walk | Stay and buffer | Booking/latest time | Status and source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add only the relevant lodging base, meals, cost, weather, photography, return, and fallback. A short transfer or high-level day outline need not fill every column.

### Comparison

State the criteria, compare applicable conditions, complete time, complete cost, convenience friction, main risk, and irreversible downside on the same basis, then recommend. Do not present incompatible price or duration definitions as comparable.

### Minimum trip conditions

When an executable task needs it, list minimum conditions as `Met / To confirm / Not met`, then conclude `Feasible / Conditionally feasible / Clear mismatch / Cannot yet determine`. Even without an obvious blocker, show pending and pre-departure checks. Skip this section for inspiration-only and format-only work.

### Cost

State currency, unit (`per person / room / vehicle / segment / trip`), required versus optional, price timestamp, and tax/fee basis. Summarize researched major items only; do not invent missing numbers to make a complete budget.

### Alternative

Provide alternatives only for real risks, with trigger, replacement, and effects on time, cost, booking, and return. Do not duplicate several nearly identical full itineraries.

## 4. Minimum quality check

Check only the active scope before delivery:

- Does the conclusion answer the user's current question?
- Were unrelated modules neither loaded nor emitted?
- Are date, time zone, party size, currency, and comparison basis consistent?
- Do decision-critical dynamic facts have suitable sources and status?
- Does an executable route include movement, waiting, meals, buffer, and safe return where relevant?
- Are cost units and required/optional items clear?
- Are uncertainty, failure conditions, and recheck points preserved?
- Does specialist content follow the modules actually loaded rather than a global duplicate checklist?

Outside formal delivery, do not run a project-wide checklist unrelated to the current task.
