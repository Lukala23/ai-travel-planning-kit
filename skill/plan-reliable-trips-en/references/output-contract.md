# Deliverable components and quality checks

Do not force every guide into one chapter order. Combine only components relevant to the current brief. Always lead with conclusions and place sources beside dynamic facts.

## 1. Common deliverables

- **Conclusion**: feasibility, recommended structure, key trade-offs, and largest risk.
- **Current input summary**: per-trip parameters used, unknowns, and explicit assumptions.
- **Core result**: candidates, route, comparison, or update.
- **Open items**: facts still unknown, pre-departure rechecks, and conditions that would change the plan.
- **Evidence**: direct sources and verification dates beside dynamic conclusions.
- **Photography when useful**: write it into the relevant route node; omit a standalone section when there is no real opportunity.

Do not begin with destination advertising copy.

## 2. Expression rules

- On first mention, give `English name (local official name)` when different; if the user needs another planning language, also give that translation.
- Every local name must be searchable in a major map or official source.
- Use full date, weekday, local time, and time zone; expose ambiguous relative dates.
- Separate fact, estimate, inference, and recommendation.
- Use `Booking required / Strongly advise booking / Walk-up possible / Uncertain` consistently.
- Omit modules unrelated to this trip.

## 3. Conditional components

### Destination exploration

Combine research boundary, spatial zones, transport hubs, A/B/C candidates, selected D exclusions, practical friction, booking/season, route skeletons for different durations, and key decisions.

### Timed itinerary

Use when a day-by-day or hour-by-hour route is needed:

| Local time | Node and experience | Transport / walk | Dwell and buffer | Booking / latest time | Information status and source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add route logic, daily intensity, accommodation base and nearby life, transport and booking, costs, local dining, weather, photography, shortening plan, and return only as relevant.

Default ordinary sightseeing to balanced coverage and express intensity with daily start/finish, core blocks, walking/standing, hard bookings, and priority cuts. Switch to holiday or high coverage only from explicit current intent.

### Comparison

State criteria first, then compare on identical dimensions: applicable conditions, time, cost, friction, main risk, irreversible downside, and recommendation. Normalize price and time units.

### International or cross-border

Add only relevant entry/transit, normal border preparation, credible unofficial-demand warnings, local transport, cash/exchange, payment, tax/service/tip, connectivity, time zone, insurance, law, and emergency information for the travel document, phone, and route.

Airport/airline fast-track or concierge is not a default item. When relevant, distinguish check-in, security, baggage, escort, immigration, and customs; never imply commercial payment guarantees entry or official favour. Every international trip includes a compact operating and safety card.

### Accommodation

Every overnight trip needs an accommodation conclusion. Compare areas first, then exact properties from official, major booking, and relevant local channels using the same dates, travelers, room, cancellation, and tax basis. Deliver exact property, true stay total, route and nearby walking, local food, room rights, cancellation/payment, risks, channels, and research time.

Separate statutory tax, city/tourist tax, service/resort/cleaning/platform fee, voluntary tip, and deposit/pre-authorisation. For experiential stays, state unique value, exact room/view conditions, moving cost, and weather-failure risk. Review route fit when already booked.

### Local dining

Every route crossing a main meal needs an executable plan. Summarise representative food, then place strong restaurants, small shops, markets, or selection zones into each day. Include signature dishes, local value, per-person spend, statutory tax, mandatory service/other surcharge, voluntary tip, hours, booking/queue, detour, payment, dietary needs, primary/backup, and sources. Do not rely only on a lifestyle platform, Google Maps score, “viral,” or “locals' favourite” label.

### Public-transport payment

Give fare system, primary payment, user-side setup, exact operator acceptance, fee/exchange, whether the same medium must tap in/out, physical fallback, and failure handling.

### Taxi, transfer, charter, and unusual transport

For important segments give endpoints/time, preferred regulated channel, local fare range and useful home-currency conversion, basis/date, inclusions/extras, app or written confirmation, local-language total-price wording, common anomalies, fallback, and verified help.

For carriage, riding, tuk-tuk, motorcycle, traditional boat, etc., add per-person/vehicle/boat/time/circuit unit, route/end point, equipment, weather/water, and animal welfare. Never guess an unverifiable price.

### Exchange, cash, and connectivity

For international trips, give cash scenarios and a reasonable amount, exchange/ATM channel and all costs, first-arrival payment, and a comparison of roaming, manufacturer travel data, travel eSIM, and local SIM by compatibility, coverage, package, activation, limitations, primary/fallback, and offline backup.

### Photography

Scan the whole route. For core and ordinary popular sights with reproducible composition, give concise position, direction, framing, people placement, and clutter avoidance even when photography does not change the route. Give a solid memory-photo method for high travel value but ordinary visual value.

Use `Must shoot / Worth shooting / Record only / Not worth a photo detour`. Expand must-shoot and important worth-shoot items with position, light, phone composition, time cost, restrictions, and fallback. Default to phone/pocket equipment and aesthetics over gear.

Attach one to three real sourced references for priority points, and at least one for an ordinary famous place when text cannot explain the composition. Give original source, creator/organisation where available, verification date, and what to learn. Mark drone, long lens, heavy edit, rare season, or non-public position. Put a worthwhile and safe early start in the main route; keep long wait, major detour, or sacrificed core experience in a separate photography upgrade.

### Hiking or outdoor

Add a safety summary: risk colour, distance, ascent, high point, total time, hardest section, exits, turnaround time, permit, route status, and weather. A credible track must be available in the user's hiking app or a trusted source, with endpoints and key waypoints, huts, water, checkpoints, exits, and transfers. Without reliable data, do not recommend execution. Do not generate printed maps, GPX, or turn-by-turn by default.

For a supplied track, review source/version, distance/ascent, endpoints, breaks/jumps, wrong branches, closures/restrictions, and waypoints. Return `Usable / Conditionally usable / Do not use` plus corrections and limitations; never claim safety certification.

For overseas treks, prioritise access and weather days, permits, sleeping altitude, insurance/rescue, travel medicine, medical access, huts/camps, food/water/hygiene, communication/power, cash, route-specific equipment and local rental/repair. Guides/porters are not a default outside legal or current-risk needs; compare self-guided, guide, and guide+porter by fit, price, qualification, inclusions, responsibility, cancellation, and labour protection.

### Museums, exhibitions, and memorials

Lightly scan every destination and search more deeply in capitals and major/historic cities. Treat genuinely important large or small venues as candidates and route anchors. Compare what is actually public, uniqueness, display quality, interest fit, duplication, interpretation, time, and closure risk before inclusion.

For priority venues give preferred-language support, highlights/deep internal route, representative objects and what to notice, true total time, and shortened fallback. Compare official human tour, official audio/app, qualified private guide, and pre-read+self-guided. Give a clear guide conclusion and verify current ticket, guide, language supplement, tax, platform fee, tip, qualifications, inclusions, and any “skip queue” claim.

For multiple priority museums:

| Venue | Actual core value | Fit and overlap | Language support | Time | Guide conclusion | Ticket + interpretation total | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Souvenirs

When relevant, suggest a small set for self and friends. Prefer distinctive, interesting, portable, fairly priced magnets, postcards, stationery, museum derivatives, small textiles, and compliant packaged food. Handmade/rare is a bonus, not a gate.

Give `low-cost many-recipient / mid-range quality / a few special pieces`, local and useful home-currency values, recipient, purchase place, route convenience, fragility/shelf/customs restrictions. Verify provenance only for high price or explicit handmade/limited/antique/origin claims. Do not default to valuable collectibles or shopping at every venue.

### Cost table

| Category | Unit price and quantity | Subtotal or range | Paid? | Source / verification date |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Label per-person, room, vehicle, or group, and required versus optional.

### Candidate coverage

Use only when a master candidate set exists or the user asks for omission review:

| Candidate | Grade | In route? | Reason / placement |
|---|---|---:|---|

Coverage detects omissions; it does not force candidates back into the route.

### Pre-departure checklist

For an executable guide, choose only useful checkpoints among `Now`, `T-30`, `T-7`, `T-48`, and `Same day` according to actual risk.

## 4. Final QA

Answer Yes / No / N/A internally; fix critical No items before delivery:

- [ ] Uses only current trip parameters; unknowns remain unknown or explicit assumptions.
- [ ] Loads and outputs only relevant modules.
- [ ] Dynamic facts have current direct sources adjacent to conclusions.
- [ ] Dates, weekdays, time zones, opening, and services are consistent.
- [ ] Overnight trips have active, normalized accommodation research and a clear conclusion covering true total, route, local life/food, late return, check-in/out, luggage, and cancellation.
- [ ] Experiential stays were not rejected mechanically; room/view evidence, move cost, and weather-failure risk are explicit.
- [ ] Convenience is door-to-door with bookings, effort, and safe return.
- [ ] Public-transport payment is verified for the user's exact wallet/card, operator, and fare product with independent fallback.
- [ ] Taxis/charters/negotiated transport have price, unit, inclusions, regulated channel, written language, scam prevention, and help plan.
- [ ] Unusual transport covers legal operation, capacity/equipment, weather/water, and animal welfare.
- [ ] International travel covers cash/exchange cost, exact device/connectivity, and offline backup.
- [ ] International trips have an operating/safety card; credible unofficial checkpoint demands are warned about without advising public-official payment.
- [ ] Commercial fast service appears only when valuable and is not confused with government border authority.
- [ ] Routes count movement, queue, meals, rest, and consequence-based buffer.
- [ ] Balanced/holiday/high-coverage mode follows current intent and is expressed through actual daily load.
- [ ] Hard anchors, core, movable, and cut nodes are clear; critical performance, interpretation, non-refundable booking, last service, intercity, and return margins are sufficient.
- [ ] Local dining is routed and cross-checked beyond viral/map scores with same-area backup where important.
- [ ] Statutory tax, mandatory service/surcharge, card fee, voluntary tip, and deposit are separated; unexplained-charge handling is provided.
- [ ] Every day has a start, finish, and return; booking level, last admission, and cancellation are not confused.
- [ ] Costs show currency, unit, date, and required/optional status.
- [ ] Important omissions have real reasons.
- [ ] Season/daylight and local forecast/warnings drive executable weather fallbacks.
- [ ] Fallbacks state trigger, changed node, new transport/time, and downstream effect rather than duplicating complete schedules.
- [ ] Hiking passes the safety gate, uses credible track/key-point data, reviews supplied files with limitations, and does not fabricate navigation data.
- [ ] Overseas treks cover access, safety, insurance/rescue, medical/altitude, lodging, food/water, communication/power, cash, equipment, weather contingency, and conditional guide/porter choice.
- [ ] Every destination received museum screening; priority venues have actual-display value, internal route, representative objects, preferred-language plan, total time, and guide conclusion with verified cost/claims.
- [ ] Core and ordinary route sights received a practical photo scan; reproducible compositions use phone/pocket positions and aesthetics rather than gear/popularity.
- [ ] Priority photo points have date-specific light and sourced real references with non-reproducible conditions labelled.
- [ ] Worthwhile early starts include transport, weather, sleep, and safety; high-cost photo plans remain optional.
- [ ] Souvenirs stay lightweight and practical with locality, recipient, price tier, purchase point, and carrying/customs constraints; handmade remains a bonus.
- [ ] Uncertainty remains visible rather than hidden in fluent prose.
