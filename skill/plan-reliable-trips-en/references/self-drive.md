# Self-drive, rental-car, and road-trip rules

Read only for self-drive, rental-car, or road-trip tasks. If the traveler hires a driver, return to the SKILL router before loading `taxi-charter.md`. Handle rental-vehicle protection here. The traveler arranges whole-trip insurance independently; read `travel-insurance.md` only on its specialist trigger.

## 1. Establish legal and usable conditions

- Verify licence, translation or international driving permit, minimum/maximum age, driving history, and additional-driver requirements primarily from licence jurisdiction, driver age, destination, date, and vehicle class. Ask residence or travel-document category only when local law or rental eligibility explicitly depends on it; passport jurisdiction is not a default question.
- Separate road law, rental-company contract, insurance/waiver wording, and experience advice. A rental desk accepting a document does not by itself prove legal driving authority, and vice versa.
- Verify left- or right-side traffic, steering-wheel position, priority rules, speed, and alcohol limits. For an unfamiliar traffic side, include arrival fatigue, first-day pickup location, and first-road complexity in feasibility.
- Verify low-emission/congestion zones, seasonal closures, vehicle ferries, border use, and special-road permits dynamically for the exact route and date; do not rely on a static country list.

## 2. Normalize quote and contract scope

Compare the same:

- pickup/return place, complete local date/time, billed rental days, out-of-hours service, one-way return, and cross-border fees;
- vehicle group versus guaranteed model, usable seats/luggage space, transmission, fuel/EV, mileage allowance, and excess-mile charge;
- main/additional drivers, young/senior-driver fee, child seat, winter equipment, roadside assistance, and required extras;
- base rental, taxes, location surcharge, deposit/pre-authorisation, payment-card rules, cancellation, and pickup counter charges;
- whole-route fuel/charging, toll, parking, ferry, and unavoidable cleaning cost.

Do not compare headline daily rates alone. State whether payer, lead driver, and cardholder must match and whether debit, virtual, or third-party payment is accepted, without collecting card numbers.

At pickup, document bodywork, tyres, wheels, glass, roof, visible underbody, interior, fuel/charge, and odometer. Put existing damage on the checkout record and retain timestamped images. At return, obtain evidence of time, condition, fuel/charge, and final acceptance; a key drop alone does not prove responsibility ended.

## 3. Vehicle protection, liability, and excess

Identify mandatory third-party liability first, then read the exact contract or policy for:

- CDW/LDW, collision, theft, and third-party cover, limits, and deductible/excess;
- whether `Super CDW`, `Zero excess`, or “full cover” truly reduces excess to zero, and exclusions for tyres, glass, roof, underbody, interior, keys, towing, misfuelling, and single-vehicle damage;
- unauthorised drivers, border/ferry use, unpaved roads, speeding, alcohol, misuse, late reporting, or missing police documents that can void cover;
- whether roadside assistance covers towing, tyre, flat battery, lockout, fuel/charge delivery, or merely provides a paid call-out channel;
- accident notification, police-report, authorised repair/tow, pre-authorisation, and filing deadlines.

Distinguish insurance, damage waiver, and deposit. Third-party excess cover or credit-card rental benefits may reimburse after payment, cover only vehicle damage, omit liability, or require full payment with a named card and rejection of rental-company cover. Verify issuer, insurer, and rental contract; card tier alone is not evidence of coverage.

Personal accident, belongings, and travel medical cover belong to the whole trip; never treat CDW/LDW as a substitute. Read `travel-insurance.md` through the SKILL router only for a relevant reminder or explicit user question.

## 4. Fuel and charging policy

- Identify full-to-full, same-to-same, prepaid fuel, or usage billing. Compare refill unit price, service fee, and nearby-refuelling time; prepaid convenience is not automatically cheaper.
- Verify fuel label, cap instructions, payment, deposit, overnight access, and the last reliable remote-area station. Keep receipts and a return-level photo.
- For EVs, verify realistic reserve, connector, fast/slow charging, operator/roaming, account/payment, parking or idle fee, lodging charging, seasonal/terrain effect, and at least one backup. Live availability is a departure/en-route check, never an advance guarantee.
- Include detour, queue, and pre-return replenishment in door-to-door time and total cost.

## 5. Tolls, tags, and restricted zones

- Verify vignette, ETC/toll tag, licence-plate billing, toll-booth payment, or rental-company toll programme for each jurisdiction and route. State purchase/activation point, road coverage, validity, vehicle class, and proof to retain.
- Separate road toll, device rental, per-use fee, and later administration fee. “The rental company charges it later” does not mean no surcharge.
- Check whether a tag works after crossing a region/country. Where tolling is cashless, give the licence-plate lookup and payment deadline.
- For low-emission, congestion, resident-only, and permit zones, verify whether the rental vehicle is registered and whether visitors can apply directly.

## 6. Parking, penalties, and later charges

- Verify exact entrance, booking, opening, height/length, plate registration, billing unit, overnight rule, and walking distance.
- Separate legal spaces, loading, resident, timed, private, and temporary visitor practice. Parked cars are not proof of legality.
- Explain parking discs, apps, displayed tickets, pay-before-exit, plate entry, and charging-bay rules. Give a fallback for app failure, card rejection, full parking, or closure.
- Use official authority or rental-company channels for penalties, towing, or toll notices. Separate the original amount, late increase, and rental-company transfer/administration fee; retain appeal/payment evidence. Do not follow an unverified payment link in a message.
- Retain contract, return acceptance, condition images, and fuel/charge/toll evidence until deposit release and a reasonable post-rental billing window. For disputed charges, request an itemised basis before using rental, insurer/card-issuer, or authority procedures.

## 7. Match vehicle, road, and season

- Choose for people, actual luggage volume, child seats, road width/grade/surface, clearance, weather, and parking—not automatically a larger vehicle or 4WD.
- Verify date-specific winter tyre, chain, traction-device, carriage/use duties, rental supply, fitting restriction, speed, and practice. Four-wheel drive does not replace legal equipment or braking grip.
- Mark narrow, steep, exposed, unpaved, icy, animal-prone, low-supply, or night-unsuitable segments, with a bypass or public-transport trigger.
- Obtain written rental permission before borders, ferries, or one-way return and verify documents, assistance, protection territory, and toll equipment. An open border does not imply contractual permission.

## 8. Time, traffic-side adaptation, and fatigue

- Use door-to-door time including pickup/inspection, fuel/charging, rest, border/ferry, parking search, and walking. Map minimum is not plan time.
- Respect each driver's comfortable and absolute daily limit. Multiple drivers do not automatically double a safe vehicle-day.
- Avoid first-time opposite-side driving in a complex centre, darkness, or bad weather immediately after a long flight, time-zone change, or poor sleep. Stay first, pick up outside the centre, or use transit when needed.
- Plan real rest and driver swaps. Stop when drowsiness, slowed reaction, or weather deterioration appears; coffee and “only a little farther” do not replace rest.
- If night driving is excluded, calculate latest departure from sunset, road, weather, and parking time. Alcohol, sedating medication, or any impairing activity must not conflict with driving.

## 9. Navigation, breakdown, and collision

- Save offline maps, route numbers, local-script lodging/parking addresses, fuel/charging, and alternate roads. Verify coverage, in-car system, cable/mount, and local phone-use law.
- Do not follow navigation into closures, seasonal roads, alleys, unpaved tracks, or vehicle bans. Cross-check critical mountain, border, and ferry segments with the road authority/operator.
- Save rental, roadside-assistance, insurance-assistance, local emergency, vehicle-location, and plate information. For no-signal outdoor areas, add check-in or satellite communication and apply `hiking-rules.md` where relevant.
- For breakdown, first place people and vehicle as safely and legally as possible, use required warning equipment, and call the approved channel; do not repair on a dangerous live shoulder.
- For collision, prioritise aid and scene safety, then follow local law and contract for emergency, police, rental, and insurer contact. Record necessary time, location, vehicles, damage, counterpart, and witness information, but do not sign an admission that is not understood.

## 10. Deliverable requirements

A self-drive plan should provide:

1. `Drivable / Conditionally drivable / Self-drive not advised / Cannot yet assess` with key evidence;
2. licence, age, traffic side, border/ferry, and seasonal-equipment conditions;
3. normalized rental total, deposit, fuel/charging, toll, parking, and one-way costs;
4. vehicle-protection gap, excess, important exclusions, and roadside assistance;
5. daily door-to-door driving, rest/swap, latest departure, and transport fallback;
6. fuel/charging, toll, parking, low-emission, and offline-navigation plan;
7. pickup/return evidence plus breakdown, collision, penalty, and disputed-charge channels;
8. sources, verification dates, and pre-departure rechecks for every dynamic rule.
