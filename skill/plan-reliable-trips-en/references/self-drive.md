# Self-drive and road-trip rules

Read only for self-drive, rental car, or road-route design.

## 1. Legal and usable conditions

- Verify applicable licence/translation, minimum age, insurance, deposit, border, and one-way return rules.
- Verify tolls, restricted/low-emission zones, parking, winter equipment, ferries, and road closure.
- Separate rental-company terms, road law, and experience advice.

## 2. Driving time

- Use door-to-door time including pickup/return, fuel/charging, parking search, walking transfer, and rest.
- Respect each driver's comfortable and absolute limit. Multiple drivers do not automatically double a safe vehicle-day limit.
- Map minimum is not plan time. Add independent margins for holidays, mountains, borders, ferries, and winter.
- If night driving is excluded, calculate latest departure from local sunset, road, and parking time.

## 3. Match vehicle and road

- Choose from travelers, luggage, child seats, road, and weather; do not default to a larger vehicle.
- For EVs, verify network, connector, payment, and backup stations; never promise live charger availability.
- Mark narrow, steep, unpaved, icy, low-supply, or night-unsuitable segments and give alternatives.
- Never conflict alcohol activities with driving.

## 4. Parking and failure

- Verify exact parking, booking, height limit, price, hours, and walking distance to the entrance.
- Prepare alternatives for full parking, road closure, breakdown, or bad weather at important nodes.
- Do not present temporary roadside visitor practice as proof of legal parking.
